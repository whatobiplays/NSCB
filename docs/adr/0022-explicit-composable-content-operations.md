# Keep content transformations explicit, composable, and evidence-driven

NSCB models retained content changes as explicit typed transformations rather than legacy processing modes or implicit cleaning profiles. Conversion may compose only mechanically required stages such as decompression/recompression; title-rights removal, delta removal, RSV/keygeneration lowering, update-partition removal, linked-account patching, trimming, splitting, and similar changes remain independently requested and visible in the Operation Plan, with eligibility and postconditions validated before publication. Multi-content packaging accepts structurally compatible related or unrelated titles with deterministic ordering, split output has one canonical NSCB representation plus explicit compatibility variants, restoration is based on independently provable exact reconstruction rather than producer identity, and rename/inspection semantics use authoritative content-derived data unless enrichment is explicitly selected.
